About

A small studio with a simple idea.

Velaro Global is an independent studio making Android apps that earn a place on your home screen. We'd rather build fewer things, and build them properly.

Respect your data

We collect only what an app needs to work, explain it in plain language, and never sell your personal data.

Simple by design

Clear screens, sensible defaults and no dark patterns. Every feature has to justify the space it takes.

Built to last

Offline-first where it can be, light and dark themes, and regular updates based on what people actually use.
