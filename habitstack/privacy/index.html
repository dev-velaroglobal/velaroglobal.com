HabitStack Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 Nov 2026

Who we are

HabitStack is a habit-tracking app for Android, published by Velaro Global ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information HabitStack collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. It applies to the HabitStack Android app and to this website, velaroglobal.com/habitstack. Questions about this policy or your data: velaroglobal@gmail.com.

Signing in is optional

HabitStack works fully without an account. Your stacks, habits, completions and notes are stored on your phone. Signing in with Google is optional. It adds cloud sync across your devices and lets you link an accountability partner, and it is the only thing that sends your habit data off your device.

What we collect

Habit data (on your phone)Stacks, habits, completions, notes, reminder times and app settings you enter. Stored only on your device unless you sign in.
Google account (if you sign in)Your Google account ID, email address, name and profile photo URL.
Synced data (if you sign in)Your stacks, habits, completions, and notification and appearance settings, so they can sync between your devices and be restored on a new phone.
Device informationAn app installation ID, device model, Android version, app version and a push-notification token, used to deliver notifications and keep sync working on each of your devices.
Usage and crash dataWhich screens and features are used, and crash reports with technical details (device model, OS version, stack trace), collected through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.
Partner link (if you link one)Both accounts' IDs, your first name, the date you linked, and whether you finished that day's stack. Never your habit names, stack names or notes.
Purchases (Pro)Payments are processed by Google Play; we never see your card details. Your purchase and subscription status is shared with RevenueCat so Pro unlocks on every device you sign in on.
Advertising (free plan)Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from IP address) and ad interaction data. Where required (EEA, UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent first through Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can change your choice any time under Profile → Privacy options. Pro removes all ads.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: sync your habits between your devices, restore them when you reinstall or change phones, and show your progress and streaks.
  • To send the reminders and notifications you turned on.
  • To link you with an accountability partner you invited.
  • To unlock Pro on every device you sign in on.
  • To find and fix crashes and improve the app.
  • To show ads to free-plan users through Google AdMob, as described above.

We do not sell your personal information. Your habit data is never used for advertising and is never shared with advertisers.

Google user data

When you choose "Sign in with Google", HabitStack requests only your basic profile: your name, email address and profile photo (the openid, email and profile scopes). We use them only to create and identify your HabitStack account, show you which account you're signed in with, sync your data to that account, and match Pro access granted to your email address. Your first name is shown to an accountability partner only if you link one.

Your Google user data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, protected by security rules that let only you read your own data, and encrypted in transit and at rest. We don't access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data. We don't sell Google user data, don't use it for advertising, and don't transfer it to anyone except the service providers below, only as needed to run the app. Humans don't read it, except with your permission, for security reasons, or to comply with the law.

HabitStack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf under their own terms:

Google FirebaseSign-in, cloud database, notifications, analytics and crash reporting. Privacy information
Google AdMobAds on the free plan. How Google uses data for ads
Google PlayPayments and subscriptions. Google Privacy Policy

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users. If you link a partner, your partner sees only the information listed in "Partner link" above.

Where it's stored and how it's protected

Signed-in data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the asia-south1 (Mumbai, India) region. Our service providers may process data in other countries, under their own safeguards. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Access rules let each user read and write only their own data, and Firebase App Check verifies that requests come from a genuine copy of the app.

How long we keep it

  • On-device data stays on your phone until you delete it in the app or uninstall HabitStack.
  • Cloud data is kept until you delete your account.
  • Crash reports are kept for 90 days and analytics data for up to 14 months.
  • After account deletion, purchase records are kept for accounting and tax purposes. They contain no habit data.

Your choices

  • Use without an account: nothing you track leaves your phone.
  • Access and correct: everything you've entered is visible and editable in the app.
  • Delete: remove all your data any time (see below).
  • Notifications: turn any reminder off under Profile → Notifications, or in Android settings.
  • Ads: change your ad consent under Profile → Privacy options (shown where consent is required), or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Other requests: to request a copy of your data or ask anything else about it, email velaroglobal@gmail.com.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it.

EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR). You also have the right to data portability, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time (for example, ad consent under Profile → Privacy options), without affecting processing that happened before. We process your data to provide the app you asked for (contract), with your consent (personalised ads), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and fixing crashes. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). You can access a summary of your data and who it is shared with; correct, complete or erase it; withdraw consent; nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated; and raise a grievance with us. If we don't resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To make a request or raise a grievance, email velaroglobal@gmail.com from the address on your account. We reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

Deleting your account

Open HabitStack → Profile → Account → Delete account to permanently remove your account, your partner link and everything stored in the cloud and on the device. Full details, and how to ask for deletion by email without the app installed, are on the Delete account page.

Children

HabitStack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
