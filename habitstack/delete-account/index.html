Delete your account

You can delete your HabitStack account and its cloud data from inside the app, or by email if you no longer have the app installed.

In the app

  1. Open HabitStack
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Tap Account
  4. Tap Delete account, then confirm by typing DELETE

You'll be asked to sign in again first — Google requires a recent sign-in before an account can be deleted.

What gets deleted

  • Your HabitStack account itself (the Google sign-in link to the app)
  • Every stack, habit, streak and completion stored in the cloud, and on the phone you delete from
  • Your notification and appearance settings
  • Your accountability-partner pairing, if you have one, and any pending invite code you created
  • Your profile info (email, name, photo) stored for sync

What's kept

Purchase records (that an entitlement to Pro existed, and when) are kept as required for accounting and tax purposes — they contain no habit data and aren't linked to anything you tracked in the app. Your subscription itself is a Google Play record, not ours: deleting your HabitStack account doesn't cancel it. Manage or cancel a subscription from Google Play › Subscriptions, or from "Manage subscription" on the Account screen, before or after deleting your account.

No app? Email us

If you can no longer open the app, email us from the same Google account you used to sign in to HabitStack, and we'll delete your account and cloud data for you.

Email velaroglobal@gmail.com

Emailing from a different address means we can't verify it's your account — please use the Google account you signed in with.
