Features

Small habits, stacked into routines.

Habit stacking links a new habit to one you already do. HabitStack makes that the whole design.

🧱

Habit stacks

Group small habits into a routine, like a morning stack, and tick them off in order. Each stack has its own days and time.

✨

Ready-made templates

Start from Morning Routine, Workout & Recovery, Study Block or Evening Wind-down, then make it yours.

🔔

Smart reminders

A reminder per stack, a nudge if you stall mid-stack, a streak-at-risk alert, and quiet hours that keep nights calm.

🔥

Streaks that are fair

Streaks count your scheduled days only, so rest days never break them. A 90-day calendar shows every day you showed up.

📈

InsightsPRO

Per-habit completion, your best day, average finish time and longest streak, so you can see what actually sticks.

🤝

Accountability partnerPRO

Link with one partner, see each other's done-today and streak, and send a cheer. Never your habit names.

☁️

Backup & sync

Optional Google sign-in backs up your habits and keeps them in sync. Pro syncs across all your devices.

🌗

Light, dark & AMOLED

Follows your phone's theme. Pro adds AMOLED black, accent colours and extra stack colours.

📴

Works offline

No account needed. Your stacks, habits and history live on your phone and work without a connection.

Free & Pro

Free to start. Pro when you want more.

HabitStack is free with ads. Pro is a monthly or annual subscription through Google Play, and you can cancel any time.

Free

Everything you need to build a routine.

  • Reminders, nudges and quiet hours
  • Streaks and the 90-day progress calendar
  • Starter templates
  • Backup and sync on one device

Pro Everything, unlimited

  • Unlimited stacks and habits

  • Accountability partner with cheers
  • Sync across all your devices
  • AMOLED black, accents and extra colours
  • No ads
Your data

Your habits stay yours.

🔒

HabitStack works fully without an account, and your habits stay on your phone. Signing in with Google is optional: it backs up and syncs your habits and lets you link a partner. HabitStack asks Google only for your name, email address and profile photo, and uses them only to run your account. A partner sees your first name, whether you've finished today's stack and your streak, never your habits.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use, or see how to delete your account and its data.
