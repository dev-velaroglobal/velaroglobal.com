HabitStack

Build habits that stick, one stack at a time. HabitStack is a habit tracker for Android built on habit stacking: chain small habits into routines you actually keep.

Coming soon to Google Play

What HabitStack does

Habit stacks

Group habits into a routine, such as a morning stack, and tick them off in order. Start from a template or build your own.

Reminders

Choose the days and time for each stack. Gentle nudges and quiet hours keep reminders helpful, not noisy.

Streaks & progress

See today's progress, your streaks and a calendar heatmap of every day you showed up.

Accountability partner

With Pro, invite one partner to stay accountable together. Only your first name and when you linked are shared, never your habits.

Your data

HabitStack works fully without an account, and your habits stay on your phone. Signing in with Google is optional: it backs up and syncs your habits across your devices and lets you link a partner. HabitStack asks Google only for your name, email address and profile photo, and uses them only to run your account.
